Business Case: Automated Job-Matching Agent & Application Proxy

Prepared for: Candidate · October 2026 · Status: Proposal with End-to-End User Onboarding & Dynamic Skill Profiling

1. Executive summary

This business case defines an autonomous, mobile-first AI agent and application proxy that automates the end-to-end job hunting workflow across target job portals. The candidate logs into the mobile-friendly web application, configures credentials for their chosen job portal, uploads their resume, and reviews a dynamically extracted skill matrix with customizable years of experience and last used year for each technology.

Once configured with predefined standard screening answers (compensation, notice period, location), the agent autonomously scans chosen portals daily at 09:00 AM, calculates a weighted technical match (≥ 70%), answers questions using a 3-tier cascade (pre-configured values, LLM resume reasoning, and mobile app approval alerts), and submits applications within human-like pacing guardrails.

Recommendation: Proceed with implementation. The addition of mobile credential provisioning, dynamic resume skill extraction with recency weighting, and mobile question escalation transforms the tool into a production-grade personal career agent.

2. Problem statement

  • Job seekers spend 30 to 60 minutes daily manually logging into multiple portals, filtering listings, and reading descriptions.
  • Listing applications demand repetitive entry of identical screening details (compensation, notice period, location).
  • Recruiter questionnaires that introduce novel or unexpected technical questions break traditional bots, resulting in skipped applications or account errors.
  • Resumes lack granular recency information unless explicitly adjusted (e.g. knowing whether a skill was used in 2026 vs. 2018).
  • Early applicants receive significantly higher recruiter visibility; manual searching often discovers postings 24 to 48 hours too late.

4. Proposed Solution & Architecture

1. Authentication & Setup — User logs into app; chooses target job portal(s) and enters portal credentials securely.
2. Resume Upload & Parsing — Ingests resume file; extracts technical competencies, role history, and education.
3. Dynamic Skill & Recency Matrix — UI presents extracted skills. Candidate reviews and inputs Years of Experience and Last Used Year (recency factor).
4. Predefined QA Configuration — User inputs baseline answers (Current/Expected CTC, Notice Period, Location preferences).
5. Scheduled Portal Search — Agent connects to chosen portal using stored credentials/cookies; queries fresh postings (last 24h).
6. Match & Recency Evaluation — Computes weighted match score (≥ 70%) incorporating required skills, preferred skills, and recency verification.
7. 3-Tier Questionnaire Cascade & Mobile Feed — Submits application:
  • Tier 1 (Predefined): Auto-fills standard fields (CTC, notice period, location).
  • Tier 2 (LLM Grounding): Answers dynamic questions from resume facts with confidence ≥ 85%.
  • Tier 3 (Mobile App Push): Posts new/subjective questions to the mobile UI with pre-drafted AI answers for 1-tap candidate approval.
8. Memory Persistence & Audit — Approved questions are permanently cached in Question Memory to auto-fill future occurrences. Decision recorded in SQLite.

Dynamic Skill Profiling & Recency Weighting

  1. The resume parser dynamically identifies technical skills, frameworks, and cloud platforms from the uploaded document.
  2. The candidate verifies the extracted list in the UI and specifies:
    • Years of Experience: Total hands-on duration (e.g. Go: 6 years, Kubernetes: 5 years).
    • Last Used Year: Recency indicator (e.g. 2026 vs. 2021). Roles requiring active experience in legacy tools can be filtered out.
  3. The match engine validates that the candidate's verified years of experience and recency satisfy the job description's explicit requirements before proceeding to auto-apply.

6. Benefits

  • Effortless Onboarding: Upload resume once; skills, tools, and experience durations are extracted automatically.
  • Recency Accuracy: Tracking Last Used Year prevents applying to roles that require active recency in tools you haven't touched in years.
  • Continuous Learning: When a new question appears on a portal, the agent answers it using LLM reasoning, notifies you on mobile for 1-tap sign-off, and remembers the answer forever.
  • Multi-Portal Extensibility: Pluggable portal connector architecture allows adding multiple career portals under a unified mobile interface.
  • Zero Daily Overhead: What used to take an hour of manual form-filling every morning becomes a 10-second review on your phone.

9. Key Decisions Confirmed

  1. Customer Workflow: Full mobile onboarding supported: App Login → Portal Credentials → Resume Upload → Dynamic Skill Matrix (Years of Exp & Last Used Year) → Predefined QA Setup → Automated Scan → Mobile QA Feed.
  2. Question Memory: Any question answered or approved on the mobile app is permanently cached in SQLite to guarantee zero duplicate prompts.
  3. Pilot Safeguards: Daily application cap set to 5 applications/day during initial 2-week pilot; 8–25s human pacing.
